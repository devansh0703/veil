Veil · an x402 scheme, on Solana
The amount is ciphertext. The chain does the checking.
A confidential transfer: ElGamal ciphertext plus zero-knowledge proofs — Token-2022, instruction family 27, operation 7.
Solana’s own Token-2022 program verifies those proofs. No MPC cluster, no enclave, no coprocessor in the trust path.
Every payment lands in a fresh one-time seat, so two payments never sit side by side on the account graph.
